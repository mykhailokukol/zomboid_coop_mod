    /* Glassmaking: car glass and a house window.

       Twelve car recipes, one per window part and vehicle class - the vanilla items are
       Windshield/RearWindshield/FrontWindow/RearWindow 1/2/3, where the digit is the
       VehicleType (1 Standard, 2 Heavy-Duty, 3 Sports) and only a part with the matching
       mechanicType fits. FrontWindow is the side glass in the front doors, RearWindow the
       side glass in the middle and rear doors.

       Levels sit on the vanilla Glassmaking ladder (pane 0, drinking glass 2, bottle 3,
       mirror 4, jar 5, wine and lantern glass 7, champagne glass 9): side glass beside
       the mirror, the rear windshield a step past the jar, the windshield below the
       champagne glass. Sports glass is more curved, +1 level; heavy-duty glass is
       larger but flatter, +1 crucible at the same level. The windshield takes glue for
       the laminated layer.

       Made like the vanilla Glass Pane and Mirror: crucibles of molten glass poured into
       the ceramic sheet mold. No NeedToBeLearn - the skill level is the gate.

       Every recipe here, the house window included, is made only at the Advanced
       Furnace: Tags = AdvancedFurnace is the tag vanilla's Blast_Furnace bench lists in
       its CraftBench Recipes, and only it does (the Simple and Primitive furnaces list
       Furnace and PrimitiveFurnace). No AnySurfaceCraft, so they are not in the hand
       crafting window at all. Like vanilla's furnace recipes they name no timedAction,
       and like them they burn charcoal: 2 for side glass, 3 for a rear windshield,
       4 for a windshield, 1 for the house window. The crucibles already paid for
       melting the glass; this is the furnace's own heat for shaping and annealing.

       OnTest is the sandbox switch (COOPGlass.onTest): with the feature off, no item
       passes it and the recipe cannot be made. COOP_Glass.lua. */

    craftRecipe MakeCOOPFrontWindow1
    {
        time = 300,
        Tags = AdvancedFurnace,
        category = Glassmaking,
        SkillRequired = Glassmaking:4,
        xpAward = Glassmaking:50,
        OnTest = COOPGlass.onTest,
        inputs
        {
            item 1 [Base.ClaySheetMold] mode:keep,
            item 1 tags[base:pliers] mode:keep flags[MayDegradeLight],
            item 3 [Base.CeramicCrucibleWithGlass],
            item 2 tags[base:charcoal],
        }
        outputs
        {
            item 1 Base.FrontWindow1,
        }
    }

    /* A whole wooden house window, frame and glass, as a carried item. The output is
       the moveable vanilla picks up off a wall (fixtures_windows_01_0, "Wooden Window",
       10 kg), so it goes in with the vanilla Place cursor - into any wall's empty window
       frame, either facing, with a hammer - and comes out again with a crowbar.

       There is no `outputs` block: a moveable is a Moveables.Moveable item that has read
       its sprite, which a script output cannot express. COOPGlass.onCreateHouseWindow
       builds it through ISMoveableSpriteProps, the same call a pickup uses, and hands it
       to Actions.addOrDropItem - which is still the output picker's redirect at that
       moment, OnCreate running inside performRecipe. */
    craftRecipe MakeCOOPHouseWindow
    {
        time = 300,
        Tags = AdvancedFurnace,
        category = Glassmaking,
        SkillRequired = Glassmaking:2;Woodwork:3,
        xpAward = Glassmaking:25;Woodwork:15,
        Tooltip = Tooltip_COOP_HouseWindow,
        OnTest = COOPGlass.onTest,
        OnCreate = COOPGlass.onCreateHouseWindow,
        inputs
        {
            item 1 tags[base:hammer] mode:keep flags[Prop1;MayDegradeVeryLight],
            item 1 tags[base:saw;base:smallsaw;base:crudesaw] mode:keep flags[MayDegradeLight],
            item 4 [Base.GlassPanel],
            item 2 [Base.Plank] flags[Prop2],
            item 4 [Base.Nails],
            item 1 tags[base:charcoal],
        }
    }
